# TAO OSINT Responsible-Use Boundaries

## Scope

TAO OSINT is intended for research using lawfully accessible public information.

Public availability does not automatically make every use appropriate.

## Allowed Project Direction

The platform may support:

- source organization
- public-document research
- fact checking
- event reconstruction
- public-interest research
- media verification
- archival comparison
- entity and event organization
- geospatial visualization of non-sensitive public facts
- research reporting

## Boundaries

TAO OSINT should not be designed to facilitate:

- unauthorized access to accounts or systems
- bypassing authentication, paywalls or technical controls
- credential theft or session hijacking
- stalking or harassment
- doxxing
- targeting private individuals through sensitive personal data aggregation
- covert persistent tracking of a person's location
- intrusive face recognition against private individuals
- publication of credentials or private contact information
- exploitation of exposed secrets
- deceptive impersonation

## Data Minimization

Collect only what is relevant to the stated research question.

Avoid accumulating personal information "just in case."

## Sensitive Information

If a public source contains sensitive personal information:

1. determine whether it is necessary to the research question
2. minimize storage
3. avoid unnecessary republication
4. record provenance and handling rationale
5. consider redaction in exports

## Automation

Automation increases scale and therefore risk.

Before adding a connector or crawler, evaluate:

- access rules
- terms and technical restrictions
- collection rate
- personal-data exposure
- retention
- reproducibility
- deletion
- potential abuse

## Verification Ethics

The platform must not convert inference into fact through interface design.

Use clear labels for:

- verified fact
- source claim
- analyst inference
- unresolved conflict
- confidence

## Publication

Reports should avoid unnecessary exposure of sensitive personal data and should preserve uncertainty when the evidence is uncertain.

## Principle

**Capability is not the same as justification.**

TAO OSINT should favor research quality, provenance and restraint over maximum collection.
